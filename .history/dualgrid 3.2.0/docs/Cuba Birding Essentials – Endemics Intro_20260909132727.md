OVERVIEW

Join us on a specialized birding journey through Cuba’s western reserves and wetlands, designed for enthusiasts eager to spot the island’s rarest endemics and migratory species. From the orchid gardens of Soroa and pine forests of La Güira National Park, to the vast wetlands of the Zapata Peninsula, this itinerary offers unrivaled opportunities to encounter Cuba’s avian treasures.
This program is the perfect for travelers who want a balanced introduction to Cuba’s birdlife. Covering western reserves, pine forests, and the legendary Zapata Peninsula, this itinerary blends birding hotspots with cultural highlights. With expert guiding and seamless logistics, you’ll enjoy a week of discovery across Cuba’s most diverse habitats.

This tour is designed for birders eager to experience Cuba’s endemic species while also enjoying cultural immersion. Expect encounters with the Cuban Trogon (national bird), Fernandina’s Flicker, Cuban Solitaire, and the dazzling Bee Hummingbird — the smallest bird in the world. You’ll also have opportunities to see the Zapata Wren, Zapata Sparrow, and Blue‑headed Quail‑Dove, alongside flocks of flamingos, spoonbills, and herons in coastal marshes.

Beyond birding, the program includes visits to Cueva Portales, the Bay of Pigs Museum, and an optional extension in Havana, a UNESCO World Heritage city rich in history, music, and architecture.


ITINERARY

Day 1 ~ Soroa, Cuba

Welcome to Cuba! Upon arrival to Jose Marti International Airport in Havana, you will be met by your Cuban guide. Depart from Havana with your local guide to Soroa, nestled in the forest and foothills of the Sierra de la Rosario mountains 1 hour west of Havana. Afternoon birding session at Orchidiarium Garden.

Soroa Orchid Garden in the Sierra del Rosario Biosphere Reserve is a prime birdwatching destination in western Cuba, combining lush tropical vegetation with a rich avian diversity. The garden’s humid, mountainous setting and abundant flowering plants attract a wide range of birds, from endemic Cuban species to rare migrant warblers ( Cuban Trogon, Cuban Green Woodpecker, Cuban Oriole, Cuban Emerald, Scaly-naped Pigeon, American Redstart, Common Yellowtrhoat, Cape May Warbler)

Accommodation: Hotel Villa Soroa or Guesthouses, Soroa. (2 nights)
Meals: Dinner



Day 2 ~ La Guira National Park

Early departure with morning birding within La Guira National Park ( 2+ hour) . Main targets are Cuban Solitaire, Yellow–headed Warbler, Cuban Vireo, Cuban Oriole, and a range of Caribbean endemic birds such as Giant Kingbird, Olive-capped Warbler, and Red-legged Honeycreeper will also be top priorities. A highlight is Cueva Portales, an enchanting and historic site where Cuba’s Western Army sheltered during the Missile Crisis of 1962. Lunch followed by birding in Cortina Estate, with chances of spotting a coulpe of Bare-legged Owls roosting. Late afternoon birding to the south near wetlands and fish farms for Snail Kites, Least Grebe, Pied-billed Grebe, Northern Jacana, Moorhens, ducks, and other waterfowls. Return to Soroa for dinner and accommodation.

Accommodation: Hotel Villa Soroa or Guesthouses, Soroa.
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 3 ~  Las Terrazas to Zapata Peninsula

Early departure to Sierra de Rosario Reserve. Morning birding in Soroa and Las Terrazas. Cuban Grassquit, Fernandina’s Flicker, Cuban Pewee, Giant Kingbird and Red-legged Thrush are likely species to encounter. Lunch at Cafetal Buena Vista, a restored 19th century coffee plantation, offering sweeping views of the mountains, plains and coast. Departure east to the Zapata Peninsula (4+ h). Birding stops along the way if time allows.

Arrival to Playa Larga, on the picturesque Bay of Pigs. This region contains the largest wetland complex in the West Indies, and is Cuba’s most prolific birding region, Zapata Swamp. Here we have access to excellent local guides, as well as to protected areas and birding locations off the beaten track.

Accommodation: Guesthouses, Playa Larga (3 nights)
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner


Day 4 ~ Zapata Swamp

Early breakfast and departure for birding within Zapata National Park and a morning walk along a dry roadway in the swamp to Santo Tomas village. Boat ride in La cocodrila channel, this is our best opportunity for Zapata Wren, Zapata Sparrow, Red-shouldered Blackbird. Lunch. Then afternoon visit to Casa Ana Birding, best place to see the Bee Hummingbird (world’s smallest bird). At this backyard you would encounter also lots of Cuban Emeralds, Tawny-shouldered Blackbirds, Yellow-Faced Grassquits, as well as many migratory warblers ( Yellow-throated Warbler, Cape-May Warbler, Black-throated Blue Warbler, and Palm Warblers).

Accommodation: Guesthouses, Playa Larga
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner


Day 5 ~  Bay of Pigs

Early breakfast and departure to Bermejas. We arrive before sunrise t the hides, here is our best opportunity to see Blue-headed and Gray-fronted Quail-Doves, Key West and Ruddy Quail Doves. Also the endemic Fernandina’s Flicker, Bare-legged Owl and Cuban Blackbird. Lunch in Caleta Buena, excellent area for snorkeling of just relax for a couple of hours. Visit Museum Bay of Pigs. Late afternoon walk in Soplillar Savannah for Cuban Amazons, Cuban Parakeets and Cuban Nighjar. Dinner at a private restaurant.

Accommodation: Guesthouses, Playa Larga
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 6 ~ Caribbean Sea

Breakfast and trip to Las Salinas Wildlife Refuge, the main target is the endemic Cuban BlackHawk and the caribbean subspecies of the Osprey, but the marshes offer a wide variety of wading birds, American Flamingos, White Ibis, Roseate Spoonbills, Wood Storks, plus Reddish Egrets, Great Blue Heron, Little Blue, Clapper Rail and Yellow Warblers. All walking is on dry, flat terrain (less than 2 miles total). Return to Playa larga for lunch and Departure to the west towards Havana (3h). Check in Hostal Boutique Havana for 1 or 2* nights. Farewell Dinner.

Accommodation: Hostal Boutique, Havana ( 1 or 2* nights)
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Breakfast and transfer to Jose Marti International Airport for your return flight.

HAVANA EXTENSION OPTION*

Our very popular one day/night Havana extension includes breakfast, lunch, dinner, guide services, ground transportation, accommodation (same accommodation as the previous night), and a guided
walking exploration of Old Havana, a UNESCO World Heritage site and one of the most unique destinations in the Caribbean. Havana is loaded with interesting historic sites, museums, art galleries, and now several excellent restaurants. Due a half century of relative economic isolation, Havana avoided rapid development and the loss of scores of historic residential and commercial properties during  the last half of the 20th century. Many buildings have been recently refurbished, some dating back to the 17th century. The harbor in Old Havana is presided over by Fortaleza de San Carlos de la Cabana, which is among the largest and best preserved Spanish Colonial fortresses in the world. The Malecon, Havana’s historic and scenic ocean drive, winds several miles along the coast from the harbor in Old Havana to the mouth of the Almendares River,

We hope you take this opportunity to enjoy historic Havana while you can!
