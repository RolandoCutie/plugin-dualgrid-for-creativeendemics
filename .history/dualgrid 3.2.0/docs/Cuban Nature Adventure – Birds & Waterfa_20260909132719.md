Cuban Nature Adventure – Birds & Waterfalls (10 Days)

Highlights:

Discover Cuba’s western and central region soul in one unforgettable journey. The Western Cuba Discovery (10 Days) offers:

- A blend of culture and nature, from Havana’s plazas to Viñales’ tobacco farms and mogotes.

- Encounters with endemic birds such as Cuban Tody, Cuban Bullfinch, Cuban Solitaire, and Cuban Black Hawk.

- Wildlife experiences in the Zapata Swamp, including flamingos, crocodiles, and the world’s smallest bird, the Bee Hummingbird.

- Adventure activities like snorkeling in Caleta Buena and hiking to the El Nicho waterfalls.

- Cultural immersion in Trinidad, with pottery workshops, artisan crafts, and Cuban music.

- All transportation, lodging, meals, and trail fees included, ensuring a seamless and worry‑free experience.

This program is the perfect choice for travelers who want to combine nature, wildlife observation, birding, and authentic Cuban culture in a single, well-rounded itinerary.

Overview

The Cuba Discovery (10 Days) is a multi‑faceted journey that combines cultural immersion, natural exploration, and birding highlights across Cuba’s western landscapes. From the colonial streets of Havana and the limestone valleys of Viñales, to the wetlands of the Zapata Swamp and the forests of Topes de Collantes, this itinerary offers a balanced mix of history, wildlife, and adventure. It’s ideal for travelers who want to experience Cuba’s western soul in depth, with moderate hikes, authentic cultural encounters, and opportunities to spot endemic birds.

This program is crafted for travelers who want more than birding alone — it’s a discovery of Cuba’s western identity through its people, landscapes, and wildlife. You’ll tour Havana in vintage cars, walk through its five historic plazas, and witness the nightly Cannon Ceremony at La Cabaña Fortress. In Viñales, you’ll hike among mogotes, explore caves by boat, and learn the art of cigar making, while also spotting endemics like the Cuban Tody, Cuban Bullfinch, and Cuban Solitaire.

The adventure continues in the Zapata Swamp, where flamingos, crocodiles, and the Bee Hummingbird await, followed by snorkeling in Caleta Buena and birding in Cienfuegos’ Botanical Garden. In Topes de Collantes, you’ll trek through lush forests in Russian war trucks, encounter waterfalls, orchids, and endemic species, before ending in Trinidad, a UNESCO World Heritage city alive with music, crafts, and colonial charm.


Day 1 Havana

Welcome to Cuba! Upon arrival to Havana International Airport, you will be met by your Local guide. Transfer to Old Havana. Check in Old Havana Hostal for 1 night. Explore Havana in style, touring the beautiful streets of the Cuban capital in an elegant vintage American car. Enjoy a walking city tour through the main 5 plazas around which the city was founded and developed. Wandering with your guide through its narrow streets you can soak up the
colonial atmosphere while visiting such grand buildings as the Catedral de San Cristóbal, La Cabaña Fort and Castillo del Morro. Your guide can recommend the best place for a refreshing Mojito - perhaps at one of the bars Hemingway used to frequent. Welcome dinner. and Cannon Ball Ceremony at Cabana Fortress at 9pm, a it is done every day since the 19th century.

Day 2 Botanical Gardens and Orchids

Breakfast. Departure to National Botanical Garde in Havana. Inaugurated in 1989 ,it contains more than 3000 species of plants in only 6km2. The exhibits are mostly divided by geographical areas and species.
Explore the different areas of the Botanical Garden with a local specialist to learn about Cuban Flora. It is the only place where there is a Japanese garden in Cuba. The area offers a superb birding as well, with West-Indian Woodpecker, Red-tailed Hawk, Gundach Hawk, Least Grebe, Common Gallilule, American Kestrel.
Transfer to Soroa, nestled in the forest and foothills of the Sierra de la Rosario mountains 1 hour west of Havana
Lunch in a local restaurant, buffet style.
Visit Soroa´s Orchid Garden, the largest in Cuba,  in the Sierra del Rosario Biosphere Reserve, combining lush tropical vegetation with a rich diversity. Native and exotic trees here provide shelter for snails, birds and other wildlife. The garden’s humid, mountainous setting and abundant flowering plants attract a wide range of birds, from endemic Cuban birds like Cuban Trogon, Cuban Green Woodpecker and Cuban Oriole, to local endemics reptiles as the Cuban Stream Anole.

Departure towards Vinales (2h).

Check-in at B&B Vinales (2 nights). Dinner at Finca Paraiso, at sunset.

Day 3 Vinales

Full day in Vinales, a small town with an economy based on agriculture,  mainly tobacco farming. During these two days in the Viñales Valley, a UNESCO World Heritage site, you’ll see mountainous “mogotes”, made of hard limestone left over after millennia of erosion underwater and now were emerged. As well as the real Cuban rural farmland views.

Early Breakfast in your hostal. Visit San Vicente Farm, an area rich in endemic birds such as Cuban Tody, Cuban Bullfinch, Cuban Trogon, Yellow-headed Warbler, Cuban Green Woodpecker, Cuban Solitaire.



Departure to “La Costanera” pine forest. Some of the specialities to be found in this area are Yellow headed Warbler, Cuban Vireo, Cuban Bullfinch
Olive-capped Warbler and Gundlach Hawk.

Explore the Cueva del Indio (Indio Cave) you will feel like a real explorer, since the only way to cross it is by boat, excellent place for spotting Bats.

Other wildlife worth to mention are land Snails and reptiles, since this area shelters Viana regina, a crown jewel among western Cuban snails, Cuban Cliff Anole ( Anolis bartschi ), Cuban False Chameleon ( Anolis barbatus).
Lunch in town.
After lunch we will learn the art of making cigars, as we ventured into a Tobacco farm, There you will not only visit the plantations but also learn the whole process that goes into making the famous Havana cigars. Other activities will include visit Mural de la Prehistoria, an open-air mural depicting the prehistoric Earth, on a hill’s wall.

Late afternoon optional meeting with Nils Navarro ( if he is available), the most important ornithologist in Cuba at this time, and author of the Endemics Birds of Cuba: A Comprehensive Guide. Illustrated with his own watercolors. Author too of The Checklist of the Birds of Cuba, a periodical edition updated each year with many useful information and every new record of birds to Cuba. We will enjoy a talk about his upcoming new book, The Guide of Birds of Cuba.
Dinner and overnight in casa particular.

Day 4 Las Terrazas

Early breakfast. Depart for Las Terrazas Ecological Community (2h). Las Terrazas is a small rural tourist community of sustainable development that offers you a unique environment in connecting with nature. The ideals of this ecovillage have attracted a striking community of artists whose workshops and studios are open to the public and deserve to be visited but, without a doubt, what most draws the attention it s lush natural environment,which will allow you to enjoy endless outdoor activities. Visit an old French coffee plantation and state.
Lunch in a private own EcoFarm with the chance of spotting several endemic birds, stingless bee hives and farm animals.

After lunch continue the road to Playa Larga in Zapata Swamp (4h+). Accommodation in Airbnb for 2 nights. Dinner in a private own restaurant.

Zapata Swamp is an exotic marshy area of almost 5,000 km², largest wetland in the Caribbean, with the great variety of fauna and flora.
75% of the peninsula is a large swampy area and the best preserved of the Caribbean islands due to its low population.

Day 5 Zapata Swamp

Morning birding session in Salinas de Brito, salt marshes for encountering waterbirds as American Flamingos, Brown Pelicans, Reddish Egrets, White Ibis and Cuban Black Hawk. All walking is on dry, flat terrain (less than 2 miles total). Return to Playa Larga. Visit an Ecological Station where scientist breed Cuban Cocodrile. Get involved in the informal talks where the local scientists explain the main characteristics of this specmens, how they study them, and their conservation efforts.
Lunch. Visit to Casa Ana Birding, best place to see the Bee Hummingbird (world’s smallest bird). At this backyard you would encounter also lots of Cuban Emeralds.

Day 6  Playa Giron

Trail Enigma de las Rocas and cave. Continue to Playa Giron Departure to Caleta Buena in Playa Giron. Snorkeling and lunch included with drinks. Located at the entrance of the Bay of Pigs, Caleta Buena is a translucent protected cove with superb rock landscapes and abundant undersea life. Shoals of surgeonfish and striped red mullets can be found along the spectacular rocky outcrops, scarcely troubled by the moray eels that have found a home there. This spot is one of the most accessible and popular snorkeling in Cuba. Departure to Cienfuegos city, then, short drive to the Cienfuegos Botanical Gardens where we enjoy an excellent tour with a local specialist. Founded in 1899 by Edward Atkns from New England, he eventually handed it over to Harvard University on a 99 year lease. It's a stunning collection of over 1400 species of trees and plants from around the world that makes for a delightful walk. We continue on to the seaside port town of Cienfuegos, known as the “Pearl of the South” and is legendary within Cuba for its long seaside promenade (the malecon) and the mansion-lined Prado.  Settled in the 1820’s, the Spanish Crown invited French people from Bordeaux and Louisiana. It's a city filled with fabulous architecture and is another of Cuba’s designated UNESCO World Heritage Sites. We take an informative walking tour of the town visiting many of the majestic buildings including the late 19th century Teatro Tomas Terry and others.
Check in Hostal or Hotel. Dinner.

Day 7  EL Nicho Waterfalls

Early departure to El Nicho Waterfalls(2h), praised for their natural beauty, the El Nicho waterfalls are said to be some of the most magnificent waterfalls in all of Cuba. Located within the remote nature reserve of Gran Parque Natural Topes de Collantes, these protected waterfalls are famous for their stunning cascades and colourful swimming holes. Thanks to the lush jungle in their surroundings, the areas is a great place to spot many endemics as Gundlach Hawk, Cuban Tody, Cuban Oriole, Cuban Vireo and Bullfinch. From hawks to doves, woodpeckers, parakeets, swifts and swallows, the treetops of the park are overflowing with colourful birds. There are some short hiking trails you can explore as well and a variety of flora and fauna to check out along the way.
Lunch. Free time for swimming in the natural pools by the falls.
Around 3pm departure to Trinidad town (3h). Check in Hostal Trinidad for 2 nights. Dinner.

Day 8 Topes de Collantes

The Topes de Collantes Natural Park is a mandatory stop for eco-tourism enthusiasts from all over. Its grand mountainous forests, frigid waters, winding trails, and Cuba's native flora and fauna make this natural reserve one of the most beautiful on the island.
Early pick up in Russian war trucks adapted as a bus, for climbing Sierra del Escambray mountains in a full day excursion.
Located in the Sierra del Escambray, 20 kilometers from Trinidad, at 800 meters above sea level, this protected park has a unique tropical rainforest climate that sets it apart from the rest of Cuba.
Visit Codina Park, this scenic route takes visitors through dense tropical forests, caves, coffee plantations, and hidden orchids. Along the way, you’ll encounter diverse flora and fauna, including endemic bird species such as Cuban Trogon, Cuban Pygmy Owl and Bare-legged Owl, giant ferns, and vibrant butterflies. A highlight of the trail is the Cueva del Altar, a cave once used for ceremonial rituals, and the peaceful forest glades perfect for relaxation. The hike (1km) is suitable for nature lovers seeking both adventure and tranquility in the heart of the Topes de Collantes Natural Park. Lunch included in an old mountain ranch that has been renovated as a restaurant. After lunch we visit House of Coffee, to learn about the art of brewing coffee, and all the history of harvesting in the island.
Return to Trinidad.
Accomodation in Hostal Trinidad.
Dinner in a private restaurant.

Day 9  Trinidad

City tour and travel through Trinidad's streets as generations before have, in a horse-drawn carriage that offers a different pace to explore the city's rich heritage. This tour isn't just a nod to the city's colonial architecture; it's a narrative of Trinidad's evolution, told through visits to landmarks.
Walking city tour through the main plaza and buildings. Stop a local Pottery workshop, since Trinidad was proclaimed World Artisan City in July 2018, first city in the Caribbean. Embroideries, needlework, knitting, pottery and so many crafts produced by its inhabitants. Lunch in a private restaurant.
Departure to Havana (6h).
Check in Hostal Boutique for 1 night. Farewell Dinner.


Day 10   Departure

Breakfast and transfer to Jose Marti International Airport ( HAV) in Havana.
