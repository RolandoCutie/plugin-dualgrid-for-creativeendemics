Physical Demands & Expectations

Most tours blend moderate treks, and cultural excursions.
- Daily schedule includes early-morning and afternoon field sessions; midday breaks to avoid peak heat
- Walking distances typically range up to 1 to 2 miles on mostly level terrain, with occasional hills.
- Light to moderate exertion—participants should be in good general health and comfortable on uneven trails
- Tropical sun means sunscreen, sun hats, and hydration are essential; daytime highs often reach mid-20s to mid -30s°C
- Mosquito repellent and lightweight rain gear are recommended
- If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please consult your physician before booking

*Warning: Cuban country is a place without threating poisonous animals, except occasional and rare stings from bees, wasps, or spiny/stinging plants, is considered a haven for hikers and tourists.

*Recommended gear: Sturdy hiking shoes, insect repellent, long sleeves shirts, antihistamine creams, sun block, gloves,
*Other recommendations: bring food bars.

Your comfort and well-being are our top priority. We’ll tailor daily schedules to ensure ample rest, hydration, and safety throughout the journey.


Accommodations

If you’re American, you can travel to Cuba, under  “Support for the Cuban people” license. Support for the Cuban people programs are licensed by the OFAC Department of Treasury.
